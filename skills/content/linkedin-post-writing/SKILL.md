# LinkedIn Post Writing

## Purpose

Turn supplied evidence into a useful LinkedIn draft without inventing customer stories, metrics, authority or personal experience.

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience, objective and source evidence.
2. Select one central claim or lesson supported by that evidence.
3. Choose a structure appropriate to the objective: insight, case summary, lesson, announcement or question.
4. Preserve factual precision while making the opening concrete and readable.
5. Keep proof close to the claim it supports.
6. Apply brand constraints, prohibited claims and desired CTA.
7. Produce a claim evidence map for any factual or numerical statement.

## Guardrails

- Do not fabricate customer names, results, quotes or personal experiences.
- Do not turn uncertain evidence into certainty for engagement.
- Avoid engagement bait that conflicts with brand constraints.
- Drafting does not authorize publication.

## Output

A LinkedIn post draft, optional concise variants, the evidence supporting material claims and any claims omitted for lack of support.
