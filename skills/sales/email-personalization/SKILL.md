# Email Personalization

## Purpose

Adapt an approved outbound message to a prospect using only evidence that is relevant, supportable and appropriate for business communication.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the approved core proposition, CTA and prohibited claims.
2. Select at most a few prospect facts that materially improve relevance.
3. Connect each personalization to the business problem or value proposition.
4. Remove weak, creepy, stale or unverifiable personalization.
5. Keep tone concise and proportionate to the relationship stage.
6. Produce a personalization evidence map so every customized claim can be checked.
7. Return the draft only; sending is a separate authorized action.

## Guardrails

- Do not fabricate familiarity, meetings, referrals, achievements or pain points.
- Do not use sensitive personal information for persuasion.
- Do not change approved commercial claims without explicit input.
- Never imply an email was sent.

## Output

A personalized email draft plus the evidence used for each customized element and any unsupported personalization that was intentionally excluded.
